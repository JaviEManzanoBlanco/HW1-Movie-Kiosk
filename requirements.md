The Movie Theater Self-Service Ticket Kiosk (MTSSTK)

|ID|Requirement|
|--|-------------|
|R1|MTSSTK will provide a view of available movies and showtimes|
|R2|MTSSTK shall allow customers select available seats for purchase|
|R3|MTSSTK shall allow customers to purchase selected seats|
|R4|MTSSTK will provide confirmations to customers for purchases|
|R5|MTSSTK must prevent seats from being sold more than once|
